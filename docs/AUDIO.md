# PACO original audio

All WAV files in `assets/audio/` are original procedural compositions and sound designs generated by `scripts/generate_audio.py` (Python standard library only). No recording, sample library, Candy Crush or Township music is used. The project may use, modify and redistribute these generated assets; there is no third-party sample licence or attribution requirement. Keep the script as provenance.

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/generate_audio.py`. Two distinct 14-second scores (`city`, `puzzle`) use soft synthesized plucks and overlapping pads. Wrapped notes cross the loop boundary; envelopes end smoothly. Format: mono PCM WAV, 22,050 Hz, 16 bit. Total audio is 1,391,838 bytes (below 1.5 MB). SFX: select, swap, invalid, match, cascade, win, lose, shuffle, ui. A match with chain > 1 uses cascade.

`PacoAudio` owns one looping music player and three recycled low-latency SFX players. Default gains: music .22, SFX .65. Music peaks are capped at 26,000 PCM units; each SFX is capped at 11,000, leaving headroom for the three simultaneous voices plus music. This is a bounded pool: an incoming sound may interrupt the oldest voice. No network or audio service connection is required.

Register `assets/audio/` and the `audioplayers` dependency in pubspec. Call initialize once (repeated calls reuse its future), setScene('city' or 'puzzle'), and dispose with the owner. The service observes Flutter lifecycle; backgrounding stops playback and foregrounding restarts the enabled scene. Pending music changes use a serial queue and generation guard. Playback failures are caught and expose ready=false; restart the service to retry. Preference write failures do not prevent playback. Settings use independent SharedPreferencesAsync keys `paco.audio.music` and `paco.audio.sfx`, unrelated to accounts or auth.

`SilentGameAudio` is plugin-free and injectable into UI tests. Its tests verify state and toggles, not actual audible output. Real device acceptance must check iOS silent-switch/audio focus, Android interruptions, headphones, app background/foreground, rapid scene/toggle changes and browser autoplay restrictions. Platform audio focus uses audioplayers defaults; no special ducking or global audio-session override is imposed. Browser platforms may reject music until a user gesture; ready=false then requires service recreation after interaction. There is no crossfade, runtime pitch shifting or dynamic score layering in this version.
